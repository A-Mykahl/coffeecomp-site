Privacy policy

Effective 23 October 2026. Applies to CoffeeComp for iOS, version 1.1 and later.

In short

CoffeeComp does not collect any data. No account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no servers of ours. Your taste profile, coffee log, saved beans and chat history are stored on your iPhone, and the AI that makes recommendations runs on your iPhone. The only network requests the app makes are to coffee roasters' own websites, so it can show you coffees that are really in stock, and those requests contain nothing about you.

The database is protected with iOS data protection, so it is encrypted whenever the device is locked. Because it is the only copy of your log, it is included in your normal iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup). That backup is handled entirely by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Deleting your data: swipe a cup to delete it, remove saved beans and roasters from your profile, or delete the app, which removes everything it stored.

AI features

Recommendations and the Coffee Expert use Apple Intelligence, which runs the language model on your iPhone. Your profile, coffee log and chat messages are given to that model on the device. They are not sent to any third-party AI service, and we never see them.

A future version may let eligible requests use Apple Private Cloud Compute. If that is enabled, requests are processed on Apple's servers in a way Apple designs so that the data is not stored and is not accessible to Apple or to us. We will update this policy and say so in the app before turning it on. Today it is off.

If Apple Intelligence is unavailable on your device, the app falls back to built-in matching rules with no network use.

Network requests

CoffeeComp has no servers. The app only connects to:

Coffee roasters' own websites
To show you coffees that are actually in stock, Discover loads the public product list from the websites of roasters you have logged, added, or that are on the app's featured list. These requests go straight from your iPhone to the roaster's site over HTTPS. They include no personal information and no identifier from the app; like any web request, the roaster's server sees your device's IP address. The app never submits anything to those sites.
Public web pages the Coffee Expert looks up
When you ask about a roaster or café, the Expert may read a public page to answer. Same rules: HTTPS only, straight from your device, nothing about you in the request, and the page is used only to answer your question.
The roaster's shop, when you tap Shop
Shop links open the roaster's website in an in-app Safari view. From that point you are browsing the roaster's site under that roaster's privacy policy. CoffeeComp has no visibility into what you do there and receives nothing back.

The app never contacts advertising, analytics or social networks.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, store, sell or share personal data.
  • We don't use third-party SDKs, analytics, crash reporting or advertising.
  • We don't track you across apps or websites.
  • We don't require or offer an account.
  • We don't send push notifications.

Children

CoffeeComp is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or erase on your behalf; all of it is on your device and under your control. If you have questions about this policy you can still reach us below.

Changes

If the app's behaviour changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example enabling Private Cloud Compute or optional iCloud sync of your own data between your devices, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date, and the app's release notes will say so.
