Support

CoffeeComp learns what you actually like in a cup and helps you find more of it. Take a short taste survey, log the coffees you drink, and Discover suggests real, in-stock coffees from specialty roasters with a link straight to their shop. The Coffee Expert chat knows your log and answers brewing questions on your terms.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: support@drillbyte.ai. For bug reports, please include your iPhone model, iOS version and whether Apple Intelligence is turned on.

How it works

Log a cup
On the Log tab, tap a usual for a one-tap entry, or Log something new. Add the roaster if you know it: that is what lets Discover find that roaster's real coffees. Rate it with beans and, if you like, add a taste reading.
Discover
Picks come from three places: coffees from your own log, coffees in stock right now at roasters you have logged or added, and real origins that fit your taste. A Verified product card has a Shop link to the roaster's own site. Save a pick to keep it, or tap Tried it to log it.
Ask the Expert
Chat about grind size, ratios, origins or why a cup tastes sour. The Expert sees your taste profile, your log and the same real coffees Discover knows about. When it recommends a bean, save it with one tap.
Roasters to shop
Profile → Roasters to shop. Add your favourite roasters by name, or hide any of the featured ones. Discover only shows coffees it can confirm are in stock on a roaster's own website, so a roaster without a web shop will not produce cards.
Fix a mistake
Swipe a cup in Profile → All coffees to delete it, or long-press a recent brew on the Log tab. Unsave a bean from Profile. Retake the taste survey any time from Profile; your log is kept.

Frequently asked

Where is my data stored?
Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server. See the privacy policy.
Discover says "Device AI" or shows a banner about Apple Intelligence.
Recommendations and the Expert run on Apple's on-device model. A long Expert conversation can continue on Apple Private Cloud Compute, shown by a "Cloud AI" badge. If Apple Intelligence is off, still downloading, or not supported on your iPhone, the app keeps working with built-in matching and tells you why in a banner.
Why does Discover show origins but no shop links?
Shop links only appear for coffees the app has confirmed in stock on a roaster's site. Log a roaster, or add one under Roasters to shop, and give it a moment: roasters are checked in the background and cards fill in as they are verified.
Can I sync between devices?
Not yet. Your data is included in your normal iPhone backup, so it comes back when you restore a phone.
